1. Data We Collect

MoatWatch collects only the minimum technical data required to synchronize your stock portfolio configuration between the MoatWatch Web Dashboard (https://moat.readybyte.top) and your smartwatch:

  • Anonymous Device / Sync Key Identifier (e.g., V2-XXXXXXXX) derived from the Connect IQ uniqueIdentifier API to isolate your portfolio and 14-day trial status from other users.
  • Portfolio Symbols, Shares, Cost Basis & WebMemos that you voluntarily enter on the MoatWatch Web Dashboard.
  • Optional Contact Information (Name, Email, or Phone/LINE ID) only if you voluntarily submit a Trial Extension or License Purchase request form.

2. Biometric, Health & Location Data

MoatWatch does NOT access, collect, store, or transmit any health, heart-rate, fitness, sleep, or GPS location data from your smartwatch. The Connect IQ Communications permission is used exclusively to fetch stock market quotes and portfolio JSON data over HTTPS.

3. Data Sharing & Storage

We do not sell, rent, or share your portfolio data or contact details with any third-party advertisers or data brokers. Data is stored securely on Cloudflare D1 encrypted cloud storage solely to serve your watch and web dashboard.

4. Data Deletion & Contact

You may clear or modify your portfolio holdings at any time via https://moat.readybyte.top, or request complete deletion of your Sync Key / Device ID record by emailing saharat.bun@icloud.com.
